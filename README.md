# What is Zenthos?

**Zenthos** is a fusion of *Zen*, clarity, simplicity, focus, and *Pathos*, emotion, experience.
It represents a calm yet powerful platform designed to bring harmony between embedded systems and cloud intelligence.

Zenthos is about building thoughtful, robust technology that feels seamless from edge to cloud.

It is a personal systems-engineering laboratory: projects evolve independently by default and integrate only when useful.

## Repository layout

- `projects/` independent engineering projects, with their own documentation and builds as needed.
- `crates/` reusable Rust libraries extracted from real cross-project needs.
- `infra/` future shared infrastructure and deployment resources.
- `experiments/` prototypes, benchmarks, and research that may graduate into projects.
- `docs/` repository-wide architecture, decisions, roadmap, and resources.
- `tools/` future repository-level development utilities.

## Engineering branches

[IoT](projects/iot/) and [Audio](projects/audio/) are the initial active areas. Storage, distributed systems, AI infrastructure, performance engineering, languages and compilers, security, and robotics are future areas of exploration, not committed implementations. Infrastructure and reliability are cross-cutting interests.
